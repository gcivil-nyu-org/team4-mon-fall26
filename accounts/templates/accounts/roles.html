About roles

Scorekeeper

Records scores for the matches assigned to them.

League Admin

Runs every League Admin page, this one included.

Rules

  • The league keeps at least one League Admin.
  • You can't remove your own League Admin role.
  • Site admins have League Admin access without the role.
  • A Scorekeeper who loses the role keeps their matches. Choose another scorekeeper on Manage matches.
